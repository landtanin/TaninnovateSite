Why Taninnovate exists

The most interesting software problems are often hiding inside ordinary work: a construction audit held together by notes and photos, a speaking club losing valuable meeting time to administration, or a specialist business whose website makes its expertise harder to see.

Those problems need more than code. They need someone to understand the work, choose what not to build, and stay close enough to the users to see whether the result is genuinely better.

That is the role I want Taninnovate to play: a practical, senior technical partner for organisations that need meaningful improvement without unnecessary complexity.

What working directly with me changes

There is no account layer translating the problem and no delivery hand-off halfway through the project.

Context stays intact

The person in the first conversation is also making the product decisions and building the result.

Trade-offs stay visible

You get a clear explanation of cost, risk, and scope so decisions can be made commercially, not just technically.

Progress stays tangible

Work is shared in usable increments, so feedback is based on something real rather than a long specification.

Support stays human

After launch, you know who to call and do not have to re-explain how the product is meant to work.
