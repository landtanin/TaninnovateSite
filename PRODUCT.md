# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK founders, business owners, and operational leaders in growing small and medium-sized organisations. They are often dealing with manual processes, outdated customer journeys, or software that no longer fits how their teams work. They need a hands-on technical partner who can understand the business problem, recommend a proportionate solution, and deliver something people will actually use.

## Product Purpose

The Taninnovate website explains three connected services: bespoke operational software, native iPhone and iPad applications, and clear, credible business websites. It proves those capabilities through shipped work and measurable client outcomes, establishes founder Tanin Rojanapiansatith as the experienced person clients will work with, and turns interest into a booked consultation or a useful project enquiry. Success means visitors can understand what Taninnovate does within seconds, recognise a problem similar to their own, trust the delivery experience, and take a clear next step.

## Brand Personality

Practical, assured, and human. The voice is direct and commercially aware without sounding corporate. Visitors should feel clarity, confidence, and momentum: their problem is understood, the technology will be proportionate, and the person delivering it will be accessible.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic technology-agency and SaaS-template aesthetics: abstract futuristic graphics, vague claims about innovation, jargon-heavy service descriptions, repetitive icon-card grids, vanity metrics without context, and anonymous corporate language. The site should not feel like an oversized agency making a sales pitch or a developer portfolio focused on tools rather than business outcomes.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the business problem and outcome, not the implementation technology.
2. Show proof before making promises: real interfaces, client words, and specific results carry the story.
3. Make the founder an advantage by connecting senior experience directly to the client relationship.
4. Give each section a job in the conversation, from recognition to trust to a low-friction next step.
5. Use plain language and useful specificity so visitors never have to decode what Taninnovate offers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive image alternatives, accessible form labels and status messaging, comfortable reading widths, and robust responsive layouts. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on colour or motion alone to communicate meaning.
